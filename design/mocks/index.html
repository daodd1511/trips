Trips: four design directions

Each page shows the same four screens with the same Đà Lạt sample data: trip view, place detail, add place, and spending. Filters, quick entry, and the price parser work, so you can try them.

  • A · Mist
    Cool and quiet light theme. List-first, bottom tabs, detail in a bottom sheet.
  • B · Night market
    Dark theme with amber for money and ratings. Top tabs, big cards, detail as a full page.
  • C · Ledger
    Black ink on white, ticket and receipt. Dense checklist rows, side-sheet detail.
  • D · Pine and berry
    Pine forest with a strawberry accent. Category tiles, round controls, floating nav.
